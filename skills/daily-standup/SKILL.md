Root posts the standup in `#general` at 10:00 and tags every agent. Everyone answers from the same empty thread.

## What it is for

The standup exists for three things, in this order:

- **Blockers.** Something stops your work. Name it, name who or what unblocks it, and make the ask.
- **Learnings.** You found something that changes how another agent works. Share the fact, not the story of finding it.
- **Ideas.** You have a half-formed thought worth a second opinion. Say what it would change and what it would cost.

It is not a status report. Nobody needs to know what you did yesterday. When you have nothing for one of the three, say so and stop.

## Nobody is assigned

Every agent is tagged and posts on their own. Do not read the thread first to see what is left, and do not wait for another agent to open it.

Two agents posting the same fact is fine. A duplicate costs a line; a thread nobody starts costs the standup.

## How to answer

Post once, in the thread, before reading the other replies.

- A blocker: one line on what is stuck, one on who unblocks it, one on the ask.
- A learning: one fact and who it affects.
- An idea: the idea, what it changes, and its cost.

Keep the post to six lines.

## Following up

Read the thread after you post. Post again only when another agent's update is worth picking up: you can unblock them, you know something that changes their idea, or their learning changes your work.

Do not post to agree, to acknowledge, or to restate an update you have nothing to add to.

## Resolving a blocker

Whoever can unblock answers in the thread and takes the action. Escalate to MaMa when the blocker needs a decision about scope or priority, and to the Operator when MaMa cannot decide.

A blocker stays open until the agent who raised it says it is resolved. Never close another agent's blocker for them.

## Bouncing an idea

An idea raised in the standup is not a task. Answer with what you know: a risk, a cheaper version, a precedent. MiMi carries an idea worth pursuing into a SPIKE, and MaMa decides whether it becomes work.
